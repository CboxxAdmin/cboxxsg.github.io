Why we're building Relay

Being out of work makes it harder to get work. Employers want proof; jobseekers need a chance to show it. Relay gives both sides that proof, one rotation at a time.

The problem

A CV can't show how someone works

People between jobs are judged on gaps and old titles. Companies are cautious about contract hires they can't easily undo. Short trials exist, but they rarely last long enough to see real work, and people come out of them with little to show.

Our answer

Paid, structured, and long enough to count

Relay employs participants and rotates them through five hosts for three months each, with a structured review at every handover. By the end, five employers have seen the work, and the participant has the evidence to prove it.

What we believe

Our principles

Work is always paid

No unpaid trials. Every month on a rotation is paid, and cool-down comes with an allowance.

Evidence over impressions

Decisions should rest on real work, reviewed the same way every time.

Both sides win, or it doesn't work

Hosts get real help and a real look; participants get pay, skills and a route to a job.

How it's funded

Paid for by the hosts, not the jobseekers

Hosts pay a monthly fee that covers the participant's pay, CPF and insurance, plus Relay's coaching and admin. Jobseekers never pay to join. There's no commission when a host hires someone they've hosted.
