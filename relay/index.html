A different route back into work

Five companies. Fifteen months.
One real chance.

Out of work? Relay places you in five companies for three months each, paid the whole way. Then we train you, keep paying a basic allowance and help you land the job that fits.

How the relay works

Pass through five companies, then land

Each rotation is three months at a different host. After the fifth, a cool-down gives you training, a basic allowance and a head start with every company you worked for. Tap a stage to see what happens.

    Safeguards
    Why it's different

    Not a contract hire. Not a temp job.

    Usual routes ask a company to commit before it has seen any work. Relay flips that.

    Contract or temp hireRelay
    Who employs the personThe company, on a fixed-term contractRelay, for the whole programme
    What the company sees firstA CV and one or two interviewsThree months of real work, plus earlier hosts' reviews
    CommitmentNotice periods, contract termsOne rotation at a time, three months
    What the person leaves withOne reference, if it went wellFive verified reviews and a Skills Passport
    Between jobsOften unpaidTraining plus a basic allowance in cool-down
