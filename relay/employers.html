Hosting

How hosting works

Relay stays the employer of record. You provide real work, a supervisor and an honest review.

  1. Tell us the roleWhat the work is, the skills it needs and when a rotation can start.
  2. Meet your matchWe send a shortlist with each person's Skills Passport so far. You choose.
  3. Three months on the jobThey work with your team. A Relay coach checks in monthly.
  4. Review their skillsAt week twelve, score them on the Relay scorecard. It takes about fifteen minutes.
  5. Offer or hand overOffer a job, or pass the baton to the next host. No notice periods.
  6. Hire from cool-downYou get first look at every participant you hosted when they reach cool-down.
Skills scorecard

Compare people on work, not interviews

Every host rates the same things, so you can read a participant's growth across rotations before they even arrive.

  • Six core areas plus role skillsReliability, teamwork, problem solving, communication, learning speed and quality of work.
  • Evidence, not just scoresEach rating asks for one example from the rotation.
  • Fair by designParticipants see and can respond to every review.

Costs

One monthly host fee

Indicative pricing for the pilot. It covers the participant's pay, CPF, insurance and Relay's coaching and admin.

Host fee

S$2,900 / month

  • Participant's pay and CPF
  • Work injury insurance
  • Coaching and admin

Hiring fee

S$0

  • Hire anyone you hosted, at any rotation
  • Hire from cool-down, first look
  • No placement commission

Commitment

3 months

  • One rotation at a time
  • Host again when it suits you
  • Pause between rotations

Any government support a host may qualify for would be checked case by case; Relay's fees don't assume it.

Hiring from Relay

Offer a job whenever you're ready

  • At the end of your rotationYou've seen three months of work. Make an offer and they move to your payroll.
  • Later, from cool-downEvery host gets first look at the people they hosted, before other employers.
  • Or from the wider poolBrowse cool-down participants with full Skills Passports from other hosts.
