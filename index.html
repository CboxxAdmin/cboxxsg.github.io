Most people who walk into your shop once never come back.

CBOXX is a loyalty card for shops that don't have time to build one themselves. Customers tap an NFC card at the counter, earn a reward, and you get a plain-English record of who's actually returning.

The problem

Physical retail runs on repeat customers. Most shops have no way to see who they are.

E-commerce brands know exactly who buys from them twice. A neighbourhood café or hair salon usually doesn't, because there's no receipt trail that says "this customer has been here six times this year." CBOXX exists to close that gap, for shops that don't have an in-house tech team to build it themselves.

How it works

Four things happen, in this order

Get a card reader

We set up an NFC tag at your counter. No hardware to buy, no app for staff to learn.

Customers tap in

First tap enrols them. No sign-up form, no phone number required unless you want one.

Loyalty runs itself

Points, a stamp card, or a tier system, whichever fits how your customers actually behave.

You see the pattern

A dashboard shows repeat rate, peak hours, and who's about to stop coming back.
