Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for jobseekers and employers. Can't find yours? Ask us at an info session or through the contact form.

For jobseekers

Who pays me during the rotations?

Seaport does. You're employed by Seaport for the whole programme and paid monthly. Hosts pay Seaport a host fee.

How much will I earn?

Indicatively S$2,000 a month on rotation and an S$800 training allowance in cool-down. Final rates would be in your offer letter.

Can I leave if I get a job?

Yes, at any time. Taking a job is the goal of the programme.

What happens in cool-down?

Up to three months of training aimed at the gaps your reviews show, a basic allowance, and job matching with your five hosts getting first look.

Do I need experience?

No. Seaport is for people with any background, including fresh graduates and people returning after a break.

What if I have caring duties or a health condition?

Tell your coach at the start. We plan rotations and reasonable adjustments around them.

For employers

Who employs the participant?

Seaport is the employer of record. You provide the work, a supervisor and a skills review.

What does it cost?

Indicatively S$2,900 a month per participant, covering their pay, CPF, insurance and Seaport's support. There's no fee to hire someone you've hosted.

How long is a commitment?

One rotation: three months. You can host again whenever it suits you.

Can we hire a participant early?

Yes. You can offer a job at the end of your rotation, and you get first look again when they reach cool-down.

What kind of roles work best?

Roles with real, repeatable work and a supervisor who can give feedback: operations, admin, customer service, logistics, F&B, retail, events, and more.

Can participants replace our staff?

No. Participants add to your team; they can't be used to cover roles you would otherwise fill or to replace existing staff.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we'll reply within two working days.
