See the work first. Then decide.

Host a Seaport participant for three months on real tasks, review their skills, and hire the people who fit, without a contract hire.

Hosting

How hosting works

Seaport stays the employer of record. You provide real work, a supervisor and an honest review.

  1. Tell us the roleWhat the work is, the skills it needs and when a rotation can start.
  2. Meet your matchWe send a shortlist with each person's Skills Passport so far. You choose.
  3. Three months on the jobThey work with your team. A Seaport coach checks in monthly.
  4. Review their skillsAt week twelve, score them on the Seaport scorecard. It takes about fifteen minutes.
  5. Hire whenever you're sureOffer a job at any point, even in week three. If you don't, they pass to the next host at month three. No notice periods.
  6. Hire from cool-downYou get first look at every participant you hosted when they reach cool-down.
Skills scorecard

Compare people on work, not interviews

Every host rates the same things, so you can read a participant's growth across rotations before they even arrive.

  • Six core areas plus role skillsReliability, teamwork, problem solving, communication, learning speed and quality of work.
  • Evidence, not just scoresEach rating asks for one example from the rotation.
  • Fair by designParticipants see and can respond to every review.

Costs

One monthly host fee

Indicative pricing for the pilot. It covers the participant's pay, CPF, insurance and Seaport's coaching and admin.

Host fee

S$2,900 / month

  • Participant's pay and CPF
  • Work injury insurance
  • Coaching and admin

Hiring fee

S$0

  • Hire anyone you host, at any point
  • Hire from cool-down, first look
  • No placement commission

Commitment

3 months

  • One rotation at a time
  • Host again when it suits you
  • Pause between rotations

Any government support a host may qualify for would be checked case by case; Seaport's fees don't assume it.

Hiring from Seaport

Offer a job whenever you're ready

  • Any time during your rotationNo need to wait for month three or for the programme to finish. Make an offer the moment you're sure; they move to your payroll and leave the programme that day.
  • Later, from cool-downEvery host gets first look at the people they hosted, before other employers.
  • Or from the wider poolBrowse cool-down participants with full Skills Passports from other hosts.
