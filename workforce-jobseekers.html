Get back to work, and keep the momentum

Seaport pays you to work at five companies in fifteen months, then trains you and pays a basic allowance while you find the right job.

Your journey

From application to your next job

  1. ApplyTen-minute form. Tell us your work history and what you'd like to do next.
  2. Skills conversationA coach meets you to map your skills, goals and any support you need.
  3. Match to five hostsWe plan your five rotations: one familiar, the rest stretching you further.
  4. Work the relayThree months per host, paid monthly by Seaport. A skills review at the end of each.
  5. Cool-downUp to three months of training aimed at your gaps, on a basic allowance.
  6. Land the jobAny host can hire you at any point, even in your first rotation. Most people won't need all five.

Who can join

Seaport Workforce is for people between jobs

  • Currently not workingUnemployed, retrenched or back after a career break.
  • Able to work full timeAround 44 hours a week at host sites, with standard leave.
  • Open to new placesFive hosts means five teams. We'll plan moves with you.
  • Any age, any backgroundFresh graduates, mid-career, and people returning to work are all welcome.

Final eligibility rules, including residency requirements, would be set before launch.

Questions

Jobseeker FAQs

All FAQs
Can I leave the programme if I get a job?

Yes, any time. That's the goal. If a host or another employer offers you a job, you can take it and finish early.

Do I get to choose the companies?

You and your coach plan the five rotations together. We aim for one familiar field, one new one, and three that build towards the jobs you want.

What if a rotation isn't working out?

Talk to your coach. We can adjust tasks, support you, or move you to another host if it isn't fair or safe.

Your next job could start with your next rotation

Apply in ten minutes. A coach will call you within five working days.
