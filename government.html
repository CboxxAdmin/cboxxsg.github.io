What CBOXX's tap data could be useful for, and what it isn't yet.

CBOXX is an early-stage discovery and loyalty product for independent Singapore retailers, pairing a swipe-to-discover app with an NFC loyalty card. This page is a brief for anyone in government who's landed here.

Where we actually are

Early stage, being upfront about it

We're not a pre-approved vendor under any grant scheme, and we have no formal data-sharing arrangement with IMDA, URA, STB, or MTI. If that would help your agency, we're open to the conversation.

If the network grows, this is the kind of signal it would produce

01

Footfall by district and hour

Tap volume per merchant, aggregated by area and time of day. A live, bottom-up view that today mostly gets estimated through surveys.

02

Cross-category spending patterns

How the same customer moves between F&B, retail, and services within a neighbourhood. Visible once enough merchants in one area are on the network.

03

Repeat-visit trends over time

A rolling view of whether independent retail in a given area is retaining customers or losing them, month over month.

04

Swipe interest vs. actual footfall

Where declared interest (a swipe right) does and doesn't turn into a real visit. A gap that's normally invisible until someone walks in.

None of this is available yet at a scale worth reporting on. We're a handful of merchants in, not a network. It's the direction the data points if the network grows, described honestly rather than dressed up.
