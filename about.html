About

A small team, working on two connected problems.

CBOXX started because we kept noticing the same thing across the shops we spent time in ourselves: good products, friendly owners, no way for people nearby to discover them, and no system for turning a first visit into a habit. Everything sold to SMEs for this was either built for chains with a marketing department, or too complicated to set up over a weekend.

0%
Why this, why now

of Singapore retail still happens in physical stores.

Almost none of the tooling built over the last decade was aimed at that reality, because most loyalty and customer-data infrastructure was built for e-commerce, where every purchase already comes with an account and a receipt trail. A neighbourhood shop has neither.

We started by asking what the simplest version of e-commerce-grade discovery and loyalty would look like for a shop with no engineering team: one swipe to get found, one tap to be remembered, and a dashboard that answers one question: are people coming back?

Meet the founder

Hakim Osman

Founder & CEO

"I kept walking into shops I really liked, the kind with a great owner and real character, and then just... never going back. Not on purpose. I'd just forget. So we built the simplest thing we could think of to fix that, and we stuck around after launch to make sure it actually helps the person running the shop, not just looks good in a pitch deck."

It's a small team of us here in Singapore, splitting the work between building the product, running the day-to-day, and actually talking to merchants ourselves. If something's confusing or breaks, you're messaging one of us directly, not a support queue.
