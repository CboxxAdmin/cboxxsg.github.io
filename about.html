About

A small team, working on one problem.

CBOXX started because we kept noticing the same thing across the shops we spent time in ourselves: good products, friendly owners, and no system for turning a first visit into a habit. Everything sold to SMEs for this was either built for chains with a marketing department, or too complicated to set up over a weekend.

Why this, why now

86.9% of Singapore retail still happens in physical stores.

Almost none of the tooling built over the last decade was aimed at that reality, because most loyalty and customer-data infrastructure was built for e-commerce, where every purchase already comes with an account and a receipt trail. A neighbourhood shop has neither.

We started by asking what the simplest version of e-commerce-grade loyalty would look like for a shop with no engineering team: one card, one tap, and a dashboard that answers one question: are people coming back?

What we believe

A short list of things we won't compromise on

Setup takes a day, not a quarter

If it needs a developer on your side, we've failed at the one job we have.

We don't sell your customers' data

Consent is explicit. What we collect is used to help your shop, not to build a data product we sell elsewhere.

We stay after the sale

We'd rather have fewer merchants who we actually help than a long list of accounts nobody's using.
