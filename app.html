The problem

Bookmarking a shop never actually works.

You walk past somewhere that looks good, mean to come back, and never do. There's no easy way to keep track of shops you like, near where you are.

WITHOUT CBOXX

A screenshot you'll never find again, or a name you half-remember. The shop stays a stranger.

WITH CBOXX

Swipe right once, get directions on the spot. Tap in, and it remembers you're a regular from then on.

Four things happen, in this order

1

Open and swipe

See shops near where you actually are right now, picked based on what you tend to swipe right on.

2

Swipe right

Like what you see, swipe right, and directions open immediately. No need to jump to another app.

3

Tap in

At the counter, tap your card. First tap sets you up, no account needed.

4

Get remembered

Points, a stamp, or a tier builds automatically. Come back and the shop already knows it's you.

Why swipe

The same tap works across every shop on the network, each one remembers you separately.
