Choose a model

Three ways to run loyalty. Switch anytime.

Points

Customers earn points per tap and redeem them for whatever reward you set: a free item, a discount, an exclusive offer. You decide the rate and the reward.

Best for: cafés, bubble tea, nail bars, salons, shops customers visit weekly

Stamp card

The digital version of the paper stamp card, minus the lost cards and the fake stamps. Set how many taps earn a reward.

Best for: F&B, coffee, desserts, hawker stalls

Tiered membership

Bronze, Silver, Gold. Customers move up by visiting more, and your best customers get your best perks.

Best for: retail, beauty, healthcare, fitness
